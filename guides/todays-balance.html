Guide

Read Today's balance

The Budgets tab answers one question: how much can I spend today and still be on pace? Here is how to read every number on it.

About 3 minutes 6 steps

Step by step

One screen at a time

  1. 1

    Start with Today's balance

    The large figure at the top is Today's balance across every budget included in the summary. It is what you can spend today without falling behind.

    It is a planning guide. The money itself lives in Accounts.

  2. 2

    Open Daily allowance

    Tap Daily allowance. This is what your budgets add to Today's balance each day, broken down by budget. Tap it again to close.

  3. 3

    Open Spent today

    Tap Spent today to see which budgets today's spending came out of.

  4. 4

    Tap a budget to see its days

    Each budget has its own Today's balance, its limit, and what you have spent this period.

    Below that is a day-by-day list. Every day starts by adding the daily allowance, then subtracts what you spent. The small figure under each line is the balance after it.

  5. 5

    Expand the quiet days

    Days with no spending are grouped into one line to keep the list short. Tap the group to see each day and how the balance built up.

    This is the idea behind the app. A day you spend nothing is a day the allowance carries forward.

  6. 6

    Look at Tomorrow

    Back on the list, each budget shows Tomorrow: what its balance will be tomorrow if you spend nothing more today. Use it to decide whether something can wait a day.
