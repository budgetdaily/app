Guide

Record spending in a few taps

Today's balance is only as good as what you record. Quick entry is built so that logging a coffee takes less time than paying for it.

About 2 minutes 6 steps

Watch it

  2. Type the amount
  3. Tap a category to save
  4. Hold a category to add details
  5. See it in the account and the budget

Step by step

One screen at a time

  1. 1

    Tap the minus on the account you paid from

    Open Accounts. Every account has a red minus on the left and a green plus on the right. Minus records money going out, plus records money coming in.

  2. 2

    Type the amount

    The number pad is already open. Type the amount the way it appears on your receipt.

  3. 3

    Tap the category

    That is the whole entry. The expense is saved the moment you tap a category, and the account balance drops straight away.

    Made a mistake? Tap Undo on the notice at the bottom before it disappears.

  4. 4

    Hold the category when you need more

    Press and hold a category to open the full form. Add a note, change the date, or switch on options such as Recurring, Shared expense or Spread across days.

    Tap Save Transaction when you are done.

  5. 5

    Check it in the account

    Tap an account to see its transactions, newest first, each with the balance after it. Tap any transaction to edit it.

  6. 6

    See what it did to your budget

    Open Budgets. Spent today has gone up, and the budget for that category now shows a lower Today's balance.
